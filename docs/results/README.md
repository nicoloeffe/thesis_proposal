# Published Experiment 01 results

This directory contains lightweight, Git-versioned copies of the reports and
machine-readable summaries needed for scientific review. The numerical result
tables remain frozen. Later report-only revisions are generated from those
hashed inputs and are tracked separately from the original execution manifests.

- [`phase1/REPORT_EXPERIMENT_01.md`](phase1/REPORT_EXPERIMENT_01.md): corrected
  Phase I report and narrative changelog;
- [`phase2/REPORT_EXPERIMENT_01_PHASE2.md`](phase2/REPORT_EXPERIMENT_01_PHASE2.md):
  spectral localization report, summary, metadata and figures;
- [`phase3r/REPORT_EXPERIMENT_01_PHASE3.md`](phase3r/REPORT_EXPERIMENT_01_PHASE3.md):
  compute-feasible reader/conditioning diagnostic, protocol amendment and
  figures;
- [`token_role/REPORT_EXPERIMENT_01_TOKEN_ROLE.md`](token_role/REPORT_EXPERIMENT_01_TOKEN_ROLE.md):
  dimension-matched structured role-Haar corrective diagnostic;
- [`f16/REPORT_EXPERIMENT_01_F16.md`](f16/REPORT_EXPERIMENT_01_F16.md):
  completed label-matched supervised dose-response diagnostic;
- [`REPORT_REVISION_PROVENANCE_20260825.json`](REPORT_REVISION_PROVENANCE_20260825.json):
  frozen scientific identities and hashes of the corrected narrative layer.
- [`CHANGELOG_REPORT_REVISION_20260825.md`](CHANGELOG_REPORT_REVISION_20260825.md):
  precise narrative and diagnostic changes across all three phases and the
  research note.

Large result tables, sufficient-statistic arrays, model shards and the
production bundle remain outside Git. Small claim and report-metric tables are
included where they are needed to audit the prose. `SHA256SUMS` records the
integrity of the publication subset.
